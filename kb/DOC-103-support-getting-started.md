## Create your service workspace
Open Support and choose Create service workspace. Name it after the team that handles requests, such as IT or HR.

## Configure a request channel
A request channel is where people submit requests. Choose Portal, Email or Chat widget in Channel settings. Each channel needs a name and a default queue. See [[DOC-110]] for channel setup details.

## Submit your first request
Use the portal or the email address from Channel settings to send a test request. It appears in the queue within a minute.

## Resolve your first request
Open the request, add a reply and choose Resolve. Resolving a request closes the loop and shows the workflow works end to end.
