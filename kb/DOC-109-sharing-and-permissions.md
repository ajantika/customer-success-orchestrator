## Share with a person
Open the page, choose Share, type a name or email and pick View, Comment or Edit.

## Share with a group
After the directory is connected, search for a group name in the Share dialog. Everyone in the group receives access.

## Check who has access
Choose Share, then Access list. Remove access with the minus icon.
