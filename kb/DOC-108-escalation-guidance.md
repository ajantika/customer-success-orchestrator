## When to hand off to a person
Hand off when the customer has already tried the documented steps and the problem persists, when the documentation does not cover the question, or when the issue involves data loss or access to another account.

## Which team owns what
Integration and configuration problems go to Technical Support. Questions about goals, rollout plans or training go to Customer Success.

## What a handoff must include
The customer goal, the product and milestone affected, the guidance already sent, the steps the customer says they tried, and links to the conversation. This prevents the customer repeating themselves.

## What an automated assistant must not do
It must not change billing, subscriptions or account permissions, and it must not promise a fix date.
