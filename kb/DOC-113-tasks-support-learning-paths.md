## Tasks Fundamentals
Course CRS-201. A 40-minute self-paced course on workspaces, projects and tasks. Free. No prerequisites.

## Tasks: Planning with Projects
Course CRS-202. A 75-minute self-paced module on planning, owners and due dates for project leads. Free. Prerequisite: Tasks Fundamentals (CRS-201).

## Support Workspace Setup
Course CRS-301. A 40-minute self-paced course on creating a service workspace and request channels for administrators. Free. No prerequisites.

## Support: Building Request Workflows
Course CRS-302. A 2-hour live workshop on queues, routing and resolution workflows for service managers. Paid. Prerequisite: Support Workspace Setup (CRS-301).
