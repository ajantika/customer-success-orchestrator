## Directory sync limits
Directory groups with more than 5,000 members are skipped. Nested groups deeper than three levels are not expanded. Sync runs at most once per hour.

## Sharing limits
A page can be shared with up to 200 people individually. For larger audiences, share with a directory group.

## Not supported
Custom single sign-on protocols outside the supported directory types, on-premise installs, and bulk import from third-party tools are not supported in this release.
