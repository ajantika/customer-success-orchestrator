## What the workflow is
Teams write pages from a shared template, request a review from a named owner, publish, and share to a group so others can find and reuse the page.

## Signs the workflow is not yet embedded
Pages stay as private drafts, few pages are shared with groups, reviews are not requested, and activity concentrates in one or two people. These are prompts for a conversation, not proof that people need training: the cause may be priorities, ownership or tooling.

## Questions to ask the customer
Which teams own which pages? Who reviews? What stops people sharing pages? Has anyone tried the template flow? Would a short working session help more than a course?
