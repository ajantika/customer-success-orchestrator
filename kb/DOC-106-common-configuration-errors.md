## Invitations not received
Check spam folders and confirm the email address has no typos. Invitations can be resent from Members. Some mail systems quarantine automated invitations; ask your mail admin to allow the sender domain.

## Page visible only to its author
New pages are private drafts until they are published. Publish the page, then share it with a person or group.

## Request channel not receiving requests
Confirm the channel is switched on and has a default queue. For email channels, confirm the forwarding rule points to the address shown in Channel settings.

## Permission denied when changing settings
Only workspace owners and admins can change integrations and channel settings. Ask an owner to grant admin rights.
