## Create your Docs workspace
Open Docs and choose Create workspace. A workspace holds spaces, pages and shared templates. The creator becomes the workspace owner.

## Connect your employee directory
If your company uses an employee directory for sign-in and groups, connect it before inviting people. Go to Workspace settings, then Integrations, then Directory, and choose Connect directory. You need a directory admin to approve the connection. See DOC-104 for the full steps.

## Publish your first document
Create a page from a template, write a title and some content, then choose Publish. Drafts are private until published. Publishing is the milestone that shows content is live.

## Share a document with another person
Open a published page, choose Share and add a teammate or group. The teammate receives a notification and the page appears in their Shared with me list. Sharing is the milestone that shows Docs is used collaboratively.
