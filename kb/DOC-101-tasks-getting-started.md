## Create your Tasks workspace
Open Tasks and choose Create workspace. Name the workspace after your team. The person who creates it becomes the workspace owner and can change settings later.

## Invite team members
Open Workspace settings, then Members, then Invite. Add email addresses separated by commas. Invitations expire after 14 days. Invited people join as Members unless you choose Admin.

## Create your first project
Choose New project, pick a template (Kanban, List or Sprint) and give the project a name. Projects hold tasks, due dates and assignees.

## Complete your first task
Open a project, add a task, assign it and move it to Done. Completing one task is the milestone that shows the team has started working in Tasks.
